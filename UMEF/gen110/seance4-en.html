Session 4 · Tuesday 3 November 2026 · 13:30–16:45 · GEN 110

Agents that work together

What we do today. You learn to make several AIs work together, like a small team. One AI works out a shop’s sales. A second writes the message for the owner. A third checks the numbers. Then a competition: who finds the most traps hidden in the file, by using the agents well?

Why this matters to you. One AI asked to do everything often gets things wrong — and nobody notices. When each AI has one job and another one checks, there are fewer mistakes, and you know where they are. Knowing how to organise such a team, and when a human must decide, is a skill employers look for.

The Wax & Co team · three cards

What you just wrote is what ChatGPT calls instructions (in a GPT), Gemini a Gem, Claude a Project: a text the model reads before every message. The model does not change; only what it reads first changes. An “agent team” is therefore the same model, several times, with different cards — and results handed from one to the next.

A good card answers six questions: who are you, what do you do, what do you receive, what do you return, what do you never do, who do you hand on to.

The rules

The same Monday report, two ways
Try it here · 2 · the simulator
▶ Do this · 5 min
  1. First write your prediction: each agent is right 90% of the time; with five agents in a chain, how many correct reports out of 100? The sliders unlock afterwards.
  2. Move the three sliders. Set the checker to 80%: how many reports does it catch?
An error at any link spoils the report

If each agent is right 9 times in 10, two agents in a row are right 0.9 × 0.9 = 81 times in 100, five agents 59 times. Errors do not cancel out, they pile up. A checker that recomputes from the file catches part of the wrong reports; a checker that only sees the message catches almost nothing — it checks that the text is consistent, not that it is true.
