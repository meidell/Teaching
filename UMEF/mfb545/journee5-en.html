The customer and the product · screens 1 and 3

A · 15 min · the customer

A person, not a target group

In Senegal the informal economy is close to 90% of activity and only about 20% of its workers had pension coverage before the agreements signed in 2026 to bring them into IPRES (source: Le Soleil, May 2026 — a figure to quote with its source, not as a certainty). The self-employed and informal workers contribute to a pension only on a voluntary basis (OECD, Financement de la protection sociale au Sénégal). Your customer is one of these people. Give them a first name, an age, a trade, a monthly income, a phone, and say what they do today for their old age: the tontine, cash at home, a plot of land, the children. Then what would make them entrust 10 000 FCFA a month to an app rather than the tontine.

Documents: which ID do they actually have (ECOWAS national identity card, passport, none)? Your Day 1 KYC must work with that document, in under ten minutes, on that phone.

1.The customer: first name, age, trade, monthly income and what they can pay in, phone and connection, ID document, what they do today for their pension — and the reason that would make them try your app.

🛠 Workshop · 75 min

The flow of money and the technology · screens 2 and 4

A · 25 min · the chain of hands

From 10 000 FCFA on a phone to a fund unit in Zurich

Every hand that touches the money has a name, a licence and a price. Here is the most common path; your page repeats it with your providers and your fees, observed and not guessed.

What arrives in Switzerland

The CFA franc (XOF and XAF alike) is pegged to the euro: 655.957 CFA francs per euro, without variation. The Swiss franc floats against the euro — around 0.93 francs per euro in 2025, so about 705 FCFA per franc. The only exchange-rate uncertainty in your plan is therefore CHF/EUR; say it that way on the page.

1.Your chain of hands: each intermediary named (mobile operator, local bank, exchange and transfer provider, Swiss custodian, fund issuer), what each takes on 10 000 FCFA, what reaches the fund, and how long it takes.

Section 5.4 · Block 3
🛠 Workshop · 75 min

Regulation and unit economics · screen 5 and the company

A · 40 min · the regulatory map

Who must authorise you — here, and there

Three sets of rules meet in your app: the customer’s country (their money leaves the country), Switzerland (the fund and its custody are there), and the rules that apply to anyone handling other people’s money (money laundering). Read the four cards, check each text at its source before quoting it, then choose the structure.

Switzerland · the fund, custody, advice

FINMA · FinSA · FinIA · CISA · AMLA

Offering a collective investment scheme to retail clients makes you a financial service provider under FinSA: advisers entered in a register, affiliation to an ombudsman, duties of information and appropriateness checks. Managing client assets (deciding the purchases) requires a portfolio manager licence (FinIA), supervised by a supervisory organisation approved by FINMA (OSIF, OSFIN…). A fund offered in Switzerland to retail clients must be approved (CISA). Collecting and forwarding other people’s money makes you a financial intermediary under the AMLA: affiliation to a self-regulatory organisation, customer identification, transaction monitoring. Check on finma.ch before quoting.
WAEMU · Senegal, Côte d’Ivoire, Mali, Niger, Burkina, Togo, Benin, Guinea-Bissau

AMF-UMOA · BCEAO · the exchange regulation

The AMF-UMOA (the WAMU financial markets authority — the former CREPMF, renamed on 1 October 2022) licenses management companies, UCITS and any public offering of investments in the Union. The BCEAO licenses e-money issuers and payment providers. And above all: Regulation No. 06/2024/CM/UEMOA of 20 December 2024 (replacing 09/2010) makes any investment abroad by a resident subject to the prior authorisation of the Minister of Finance. A Dakar resident buying units of a Swiss fund every month is making an investment abroad. This is the question that decides your structure.
CEMAC · Congo-Brazzaville, Cameroon, Gabon, Chad, CAR, Equatorial Guinea

COSUMAF · BEAC · regulation 02/18

The COSUMAF supervises the regional financial market and licenses collective schemes and their managers. The BEAC applies Regulation No. 02/18/CEMAC/UMAC/CM of 21 December 2018 (in force 1 March 2019) and its instruction of 5 October 2020 on direct and portfolio investments with abroad: residents’ capital outflows are subject to authorisation and supporting documents, and the rules have been enforced strictly since 2019. DRC (Congolese franc, outside CEMAC): the Central Bank of Congo has its own exchange regulation. Check on beac.int and cosumaf.org.
The insurance route

CIMA · a retirement-savings contract

In the fourteen CIMA countries (the Inter-African Conference of Insurance Markets — WAEMU and CEMAC together, plus others), a licensed life insurer may sell a unit-linked retirement-savings contract. The insurer carries the licence, the custody and the relationship with the regulator; your app is the distribution channel and the interface. The insurers’ investment rules (share allowed outside the zone) then apply to the Swiss fund. CIMA Code, Book IV, to be checked.

B · 35 min · unit economics

What a customer brings in, what one costs, how many you need

A customer at 10 000 FCFA a month brings in little to whoever takes 1% of the payment and 0.8% of assets: this is a business of volume and patience — assets, and so fees, grow with the years. Do the two calculations below, then your own with your fees, your acquisition cost (Day 3: CAC, LTV, churn) and your fixed costs.

Two reference calculations

Assumptions of the two calculations: 10 000 FCFA a month, 1% per payment, 0.8% a year on an average holding of 400 000 FCFA (a customer saving for three years), fixed costs of 210 000 000 FCFA a year — about CHF 300 000 at 700 FCFA per franc: three salaries, compliance, hosting, support. Change all of it for your case; keep the method.

2.Your numbers: revenue per customer per year (in year 1 and year 5), cost to acquire a customer and through which channel, assumed churn, annual fixed costs, number of customers at break-even — and the year you reach it.

What the page contains, in this order

  3. The product The plan: payment, age, fund with ISIN and TER, the projector’s five numbers, the fee share, the payout at 65, early withdrawal (5.2 B and C).
