Fintech

Instant payments and mobile money, digital credit and neobanks, blockchain and decentralised finance, artificial intelligence and regtech: how it works in practice, how it makes money, and under which regulatory framework — in Switzerland, in the European Union and in the WAEMU area. Six applied days, with cases from the Swiss financial centre and from the markets of West Africa and the Maghreb.

The project · “Build your fintech”

40% · A pension-savings app for Africa, invested in Switzerland

Two teams of three, one brief. An app that lets people in Senegal, Congo (Brazzaville or DRC) or a neighbouring country save for their retirement by paying small amounts every month until 65, invested in Swiss blue-chip equity funds, among the most stable on the market. The app and the company must be transparent and earn trust: people hand them money for thirty years. Each team picks its country and its customer, and designs: the customer journey, the savings plan and its fees, the flow of money to the fund and back, the custody of the assets, the technology choice, compliance (in Switzerland and in the target country), funding. In line with the regulations, the project replaces the midterm exam.

(a) The dossierFifteen pages at most. Friday 20 November 2026, 23:59, handed in here, on this site.
(b) The prototypeThe customer journey, clickable.
(c) The presentationTen minutes before the jury on Saturday 14 November 2026, ten minutes of questions.

One mark for the team, weighted where needed by an individual assessment of contribution. Everything is handed in on this site — the presentation slides, the prototype, the dossier and the financial model; nothing on Moodle or the Swiss UMEF platform.
