Swiss UMEF · Master in Finance and Banking · Year 1 · MFB 545

Fintech

Instant payments and mobile money, digital credit and neobanks, blockchain and decentralised finance, artificial intelligence and regtech: how it works in practice, how it makes money, and under which regulatory framework — in Switzerland, in the European Union and in the WAEMU area. Six applied days, with cases from the Swiss financial centre and from the markets of West Africa and the Maghreb.

Instructor: Prof. Jan Erik Meidell, PhD  ·  Schedule Friday or Saturday, 09:00–12:15 and 13:30–16:45  ·  Workload 39 h in class, 6 days  ·  6 ECTS  ·  Final exam Friday 5 February 2027, 09:45

The project · “Build your fintech”

40% · A fintech for a real customer problem

Three teams of three. Each team picks a real customer problem in a market it knows — West Africa, the Maghreb or Switzerland — and designs the fintech that solves it: customer journey, revenue model, payment flow, risk and scoring, technology choice, compliance, funding. In line with the regulations, the project replaces the midterm exam.

(a) The reportFifteen pages at most. Friday 20 November 2026, 23:59, on the Swiss UMEF platform.
(b) The prototypeThe customer journey, clickable.
(c) The presentationTen minutes before the jury on Saturday 14 November 2026, ten minutes of questions.

One grade for the team, weighted where appropriate by an individual assessment of each member’s contribution.

    Prerequisites and tools — free accounts are enough

    • Prerequisites: the basics of finance (a bank’s balance sheet, interest rates, the time value of money, credit risk) and everyday use of Excel. No programming: the notebooks are guided step by step.

    • On this site: the simulators, the interactive quizzes, the class games, the API workshop in test mode.
    • Day 4: the courses « Why trustless works » ↗ and « How DeFi works » ↗, the DeFi capstone ↗, the tool “Should this be a blockchain?” ↗ and the MEV ↗ simulator, already online.
