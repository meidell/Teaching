The workshop

Your app, from idea to live address

One page, five steps. You describe, the AI writes the code, you run it here, you check, you fix. Everything you type stays in this browser: reloading the page loses nothing.

How it works, in short
  1. You choose a team name (step 1).
  2. You choose an app idea (step 2).
  3. You copy a ready-made text — a prompt — and paste it into a free AI, in another tab.
  4. The AI answers with code. You copy its whole answer, paste it here (step 3) and click ▶ Run.
  5. You try your app. If something is wrong, you tell the AI with prompt 3, then start again from point 4.
  6. When it works, you click 🚀 Publish (step 5).

You do not need to understand the code. Your job: tell the AI clearly what you want, then check that the app really does it.

1 Your team

What you do
  1. Type a short name for your team in the field below, for example the-carpoolers.
  2. Wait for the green ✓. If a red message says the name is already taken, choose another one.
  3. The whole team uses this same name, on all its devices, all morning.

2 Your prompts

What you do
  1. Open a new tab and go to a free AI: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Le Chat. Sign in (a free account is enough) and start a new conversation.
  2. Come back here. In the form just below, choose an idea from the list — or write your own in the five fields.
  3. Click Copy at the top right of prompt 1. Paste it into the AI (Ctrl+V, on a Mac ⌘V) and send it.
  4. Go to step 3 with the AI’s answer.

The prompts fill themselves in with your idea and your team’s address. Prompts 2, 3 and 4 are for later: under each one it says when to use it.

Prompt 1 · the first version (no database)

When: at the very start. What it does: asks the AI for a first version of the app, which works on one device only.

Prompt 2 · connect the database

When: once version 1 works in step 3. Send it in the same conversation. What it does: the app stores its data in the shared database, so that all users see the same data. Then: paste the new answer in step 3, in place of the old code.

Prompt 3 · when it does not work

When: every time something does not work. Before sending: paste it into the AI, then replace the three texts in [square brackets] with what really happened for you. The red errors from step 3 are already added automatically.

Prompt 4 · improve, one request at a time

When: once everything works and you want to improve the app. Before sending: delete every line of the list except one — one improvement at a time, then test.

3 Paste the code, run it

What you do
  1. In the AI, copy the whole answer. Easiest: the code block’s Copy button, or select everything with the mouse. The sentences around the code do no harm; they are removed automatically.
  2. Click in the big box below. Delete what is in it (Ctrl+A then Delete; on a Mac ⌘A), then paste (Ctrl+V; ⌘V).
  3. Click ▶ Run. Your app appears in the white frame underneath.
  4. Try every feature, the way a user would: fill in the fields, click the buttons.
  5. Look at the black panel on the right. Red = an error. If there is red, or a feature does not do what it should: prompt 3 (step 2).

Every time the AI sends a new version: repeat 1 to 4. A new version broke everything? Choose the previous one in the Versions menu.

The code runs in a sandbox: it cannot read anything from this page or your browser.

4 What is in your database

What you do — only after prompt 2
  1. In your app (step 3), add something: a message, an ad, an answer…
  2. Click ↻ Re-read the database below. What you just added must appear in the black box.
  3. If the box stays empty, your app is not writing to the database: use prompt 3 and tell the AI.

This box shows your data as it is stored in the database: one big tree of names and values, called JSON.

—

5 Publish

What you do
  1. Check the title below (it is the name shown in the gallery).
  2. Click 🚀 Publish. A link appears underneath.
  3. Two-device test: open that link on a phone. Add something on the phone, then look at the computer: it must appear within ten seconds (and the other way round).
  4. After every fix: ▶ Run in step 3, then 🚀 Publish again. Publishing puts the last version you ran online; the gallery always shows the latest.
