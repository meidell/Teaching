Closing

Which number do you trust?

A board reads one number and decides a budget. Every number on this page counts something different, and the notes under the charts say what each one counts and who reported it. Read the list below, then answer as a team.

The final wager

  1. As a team, choose the one number on this page you would put in front of your board, and the one you would never quote. Write one reason for each.
  2. Each team writes its wager in the table below: any number of points, up to the points it has.
  3. Each team reads its two numbers and its reasons aloud. A team wins its wager when both reasons say what the number counts and who reported it. Otherwise it loses its wager.

Your team says its wager aloud; the instructor enters it and shows the result on the scoreboard.

The figures, their sources and the incident cards follow the course's build brief of 13 November 2026, unchanged. A value that is not known is left as a gap, never estimated. A source named without a web address is shown as plain text.
