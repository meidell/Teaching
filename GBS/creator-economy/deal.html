1 · Your BATNA

What would your side have done if there was no deal, and what was that worth in Swiss francs? Did you think your BATNA was stronger or weaker than the other side's? Does a weak BATNA mean you must ask for less?

2 · Where did the number come from?

Each pair: you settled on CHF X. Which fact or rule produced that number? Which standards were most persuasive: the ones closest to this deal (her own fees, this brand, this year), or the general ones?

3 · Interests and options beyond the fee

What did each side want besides money? Which terms did you invent that cost one side little and helped the other a lot: a bonus per pair, a results letter for her next client, usage rights that also bring her followers, a giveaway?

4 · Agents and authority

What could you promise without asking your boss or your client? Did you say so, or did the other side assume it? Would knowing the other side's instructions have made the deal easier or harder to reach?
