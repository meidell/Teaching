ÒRBITA's five systems: what each one records, every column, and sample rows

Why this page exists. When a step asks "which system and which column does this come from?", the answer is one line of this page. ÒRBITA (fictional) keeps its data in five separate systems. The data warehouse copies from them; it never invents a column.

How to use it.

  1. Type a word in the search box, for example city, email or date. Every column that matches is highlighted, in every system.
  2. Click a column. The page copies "system · column" (for example Telematics · km), ready to paste into the source-to-target map.
  3. Open the sample rows under each system to see what real values look like, including the messy ones.
How the systems connect. Two systems describe the same thing only through a column they share:
· subscriber_id links billing to telematics: the account in billing is the rider in telematics.
· email links billing's customer table to the support desk: the desk knows customers only by email, and an email can change.
· city and week link marketing's ad spend to the rides and accounts, but only once every city is spelled the same way.
· The finance spreadsheet shares no key with anything: it holds monthly totals that someone typed in.
