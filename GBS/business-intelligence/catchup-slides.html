Part 2 · how to hand in a page, in six actions

Download the page, put it in your folder, upload the folder to Netlify, paste the address, press Hand in

  1. On the session page, in Step 6, press Download. The file (index.html, schema.html, etl.html or profile.html) lands in your Downloads folder.
  2. Move it into your team's folder orbita-memo, next to the pages already there. Do not rename schema, etl or profile.
  3. Sign in at app.netlify.com, open your team's project, then Deploys.
  4. Drag the whole orbita-memo folder onto the upload box at the bottom. The address stays the same.
  5. Open your address + /schema.html (or /etl.html, /profile.html) in a private window to check it, and paste that full address into the box in Step 6.
  6. One member presses Hand in. The page saves it in every member's record and adds it to the class list.
The Hand in button refuses an address that is not a netlify.app address with the right file name: that is the check.
