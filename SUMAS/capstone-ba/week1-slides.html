Five ideas · 2

Trends vs uncertainties

Trend

Direction predictable even if pace is not: population ageing, electrification, cost decline under Wright's Law.
Plan around it.

Uncertainty

High impact, could go either way: post-2030 policy ambition, interest rates, Chinese access to EU auctions.
Build scenarios on it.

High impact + low uncertainty → monitor. High impact + high uncertainty → your axes. Low impact → acknowledge, move on.

Two definitions, then straight to the live vote on the next slide.

Five ideas · 3

Scenario planning · it does not predict

  1. Define the question — what decision needs long-term clarity
  2. Identify key uncertainties — impactful and unpredictable
  3. Build scenarios — two axes, four worlds
  4. Test strategies — how does each company fare in each world
  5. Identify robust actions — what works across several worlds

Example axes for wind: climate-policy strength × speed of cost decline. You build your own in Week 4.

Five ideas · 4

Two loops you will draw in Week 5

Reinforcing · growth

Falling LCOE → more investment → more deployment → more scale → lower LCOE → …

Balancing · constraint

Faster deployment → grid congestion → curtailment → lower revenues → slower investment → …

Every growth engine eventually meets a brake. Where the brake bites is where interventions have leverage.

Ask: which actors on your walls sit on the balancing loop? Grid operators, regulators, communities. That is the answer to "why does the Commission's target not simply happen".

Case study

The sequel the case leaves out

Late 2023: about $4 billion of impairments; Ocean Wind 1 and 2 cancelled. 2025: Hornsea 4 cancelled; DKK 60bn of new equity.

Interest rates up 400 basis points, steel prices doubled, supply chains seized, US permitting slipped — all at once. The company had excellent forecasts. It had no scenario in which all four happened together. That scenario was visible in 2021.

This is the most important slide of the night. A company with excellent forecasts and no scenarios. Hold both halves of the story: the transformation AND the reset. Week 4 builds the scenario that would have caught 2023.
