The team report · written here, handed in here

Is wind a sound long-term investment for Europe — and Ørsted or Eni?

One report per team, eighteen sections in three phases, modelled on last year's cohort report. Each section is a box you write in; it saves as you type, your teammates see it within seconds, and one person at a time holds a section. When a phase is ready, any one of you hands it in from the panel on the right.
