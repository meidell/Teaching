WEEK 3 · HOMEWORK · ~2 HOURS · CALCULATOR ALLOWED · PRACTICE FOR THE CASE STUDY AND THE EXAM

Judge a project, by hand

Eighteen problems, in the order the week taught them: NPV first, then the cash flows of one project — Nyon Cycles' second shop in Morges — built line by line, then IRR, payback and the index, then sensitivity and two decisions. Work each one by hand with a calculator that has a yx key, type your answer, and check it. Stuck? Take a hint. Then read the full solution — that's where the learning is. Amounts in CHF 000 to two decimals; rates as percentages to two decimals; the checker allows small rounding differences.

📋 The formulas — and the factors you will need

NPV = C₀ + Σ Cₜ ÷ (1 + r)ᵗ · accept if > 0 OCF = NI + depreciation = EBITDA − tax = EBITDA × (1 − T) + T × dep Tax shield = depreciation × T IRR ≈ r₁ + (r₂ − r₁) × NPV₁ ÷ (NPV₁ − NPV₂) Payback: years until cumulative cash flow ≥ 0 Profitability index = NPV ÷ investment Annuity factors at 9%: 3 y 2.531295 · 4 y 3.239720 · 5 y 3.889651 · 6 y 4.485919 Discount factors at 9%: 1 y 0.917431 · 2 y 0.841680 · 3 y 0.772183 · 4 y 0.708425 · 5 y 0.649931

Keep at least six significant figures in every factor; round only the final answer. Financing — loans, interest, dividends — never enters a project's cash flows.
