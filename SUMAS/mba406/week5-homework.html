Value a business, by hand

Eighteen problems, in the order the week taught them: a bond and a loan, a share from its dividends, Nyon Cycles' free cash flow, and then what the whole shop is worth, by discounted cash flow and by multiples. Work each one by hand with a calculator, then check. Take the hint before the solution.

📋 The formulas

Bond: price = C × [1 − 1 ⁄ (1 + y)ⁿ] ⁄ y + F ⁄ (1 + y)ⁿ Loan payment = amount ⁄ annuity factor (monthly rate, months) Share: P₀ = DIV₁ ⁄ (r − g) · r = DIV₁ ⁄ P₀ + g FCF = EBIT (1 − T) + depreciation − capex − Δ working capital Terminal value = FCFn+1 ⁄ (WACC − g) Equity = EV − debt + cash · EV = EBITDA × multiple

Amounts in CHF 000 unless stated, to two decimals; keep at least six significant figures in intermediate steps. Working capital means receivables + inventory − payables.
