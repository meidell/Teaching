Find the cash, by hand

Eighteen problems, in the order the week taught them: Nyon Cycles' cash conversion cycle, its credit terms and one credit decision, what its stock of bikes costs to hold and how much to order, a six-month cash budget through the spring season, and how to finance the peak. Work each one by hand with a calculator, then check. Take the hint before the solution.

📋 The formulas, and Nyon Cycles' 2025 figures (CHF 000)

Days = balance ⁄ yearly flow × 365 (inventory and payables on cost of goods sold; receivables on revenue) Cycle = inventory days + receivable days − payable days Missed discount: (1 + d ⁄ (100 − d))365 ⁄ extra days − 1 Credit: break-even p = margin ⁄ (margin + cost) EOQ = √(2 × usage × cost per order ⁄ carrying cost per unit) Budget: cash + collections − payments; draw to the minimum
