Build a hurdle rate, by hand

Eighteen problems, in the order the week taught them: risk and return, diversification, beta and the CAPM, then Nyon Cycles' cost of debt and WACC, and finally the rate its Morges shop from Week 3 should clear. Work each one by hand with a calculator, then check. Take the hint before the solution.

📋 The formulas

Average r̄ = Σ r ÷ n · σ = √( Σ (r − r̄)² ÷ (n − 1) ) Scenarios: E = Σ p r · σ² = Σ p (r − E)² Two shares: σ² = w₁²σ₁² + w₂²σ₂² + 2 w₁ w₂ ρ σ₁ σ₂ Beta = cov(share, market) ÷ var(market) CAPM: rE = rf + β (rm − rf) · rf 1%, premium 6% WACC = E ⁄ V × rE + D ⁄ V × rD (1 − T)

Rates in per cent to two decimals; keep at least six significant figures in intermediate steps. The cost of debt looks forward: what new money costs, after tax.
