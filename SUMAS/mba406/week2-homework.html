WEEK 2 · HOMEWORK · ~2 HOURS · CALCULATOR ALLOWED · COUNTS TOWARD PARTICIPATION

Move money through time, by hand

Eighteen problems, in the order the week taught them: single sums forward and back, then a rate and a time, then streams and annuities, rates, a loan schedule, and two decisions. Work each one by hand with a calculator that has a yx key, type your answer, and check it. Stuck? Take a hint. Then read the full solution — that's where the learning is. Amounts in CHF to two decimals; rates as percentages to two decimals; the checker allows small rounding differences.

📋 The formulas — and the multipliers you will need

FV = PV × (1 + r)t PV = FV ÷ (1 + r)t Annuity PV = C × [1 − (1 + r)−n] ÷ r Annuity FV = C × [(1 + r)n − 1] ÷ r Perpetuity = C ÷ r · growing: C₁ ÷ (r − g) Annuity due = ordinary × (1 + r) EAR = (1 + APR ÷ m)m − 1 Real: 1 + real = (1 + nominal) ÷ (1 + inflation)

Keep at least six significant figures in every multiplier; round only the final answer.
