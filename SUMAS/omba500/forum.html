What I am looking at

Forums carry the participation share of the grade, built up week by week. Every required post and every required reply is read and marked on the same four things, whatever the week's task:

What I look atFull marks look like
EvidenceNumbers with a source and a year; AI use declared; a number you checked yourself rather than copied.
MethodThe week's tool used as the week taught it — a learning rate from a fit, an axis that passes the orthogonality test, a loop with its signs.
JudgmentYou said which scenario, which company, which number you believe — and why. "It depends" with no conditions is not a judgment.
EngagementReplies that give a classmate something to act on: a number to check, an assumption to defend, an alternative to consider. "Great post" scores nothing.

Ground rules

  • This page is visible to the whole class and to me. It is not private. Post nothing you would not put on a slide in front of your employer.
  • Name your sources. A link, a page number, a year. Document which AI tools you used and what you verified yourself.
  • You can edit your post until the deadline, but you cannot delete it. Replies, once posted, stand. Write it as you mean it.
  • Disagree freely. Challenge the analysis, never the person. Nobody loses marks for being challenged — or for changing their mind in the thread.
  • The deadline check is on this page only; if something got in the way, message me rather than just leaving it.
