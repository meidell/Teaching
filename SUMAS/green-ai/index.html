Your progress through the sessions

The four steps

Each step is one phase of the project and one part of the final report.

1 · PICKA real environmental problem you can actually see and touch. Air quality on your school run. Waste in your neighbourhood. Heat in your district.
2 · INVESTIGATEUse AI — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity — to dig into the data, the science and the local context.
3 · PROPOSEUse AI to explore solutions. What has been tried? What works? What would you build for your city?
4 · PITCH — the final report and the presentation to your instructor

The toolkit

All free at entry level. All of them can be wrong. The skill is knowing when to trust which one — and checking.

Claude · AnthropicLong careful reasoning. Structured analysis. Writing reports that hold together.
ChatGPT · OpenAIBrainstorming. Drafting fast. General-purpose ideation.
Gemini · GoogleReal-time information. Images, maps, multimodal. Connected to Google Search and YouTube.
Perplexity · Perplexity AIWeb research with sources you can click. Built for fact-checking.
