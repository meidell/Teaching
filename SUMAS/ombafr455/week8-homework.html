Twenty-two problems on everything Week 8 taught: why a capped market's total is set by law rather than by trading, how a voluntary credit is built out of an unobservable baseline, the four ways it fails, the arithmetic that converts a sticker price into a cost per real tonne, where compensation belongs on the mitigation hierarchy, and what happens when the same template is applied to nature and to retail capital.

Numeric answers: round as instructed; comma or dot decimals both work. Give money in CHF without the symbol or thousands separators, and percentages as numbers (e.g. 8.4).
