      Who does what

      • The student office prepares the transcript, checks the record and issues it with a number and a verification code. It is the only office that may issue one.
      • The academic direction signs it. An unsigned transcript is a draft and is never sent.
      • The programme manager and the advisor read the study plan to follow retakes, warnings and the graduation date; they do not issue transcripts.
      • The student sees the study plan on the student dashboard and asks the student office for a transcript.
      • An employer or another school checks a transcript by its verification code.
