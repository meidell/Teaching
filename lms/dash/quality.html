      Faculty and graduates

      Faculty qualification profile, in AACSB's four categories

      These four categories come from AACSB, the American business-school accreditor, not from the Swiss framework: academics hold a doctorate, practitioners do not, and "scholarly" means they publish research. The Swiss standard 7.2 asks only that academic qualifications are appropriate to the type of institution; the faculty qualification list (evidence E24) is what answers it.
