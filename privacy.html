Privacy

janerikmeidell.com and the Courses app · last updated 17 October 2026

This site and its companion app are run by Jan Erik Meidell, an independent lecturer, for the students of his courses. They store as little as a course needs, and nothing is sold, shared with advertisers or used for anything other than teaching the course you are enrolled in.

What is stored

DataWhy
Your name and a six-digit course codeSo you can pick up your progress on another device. The code is chosen or given when you first sign in; there is no e-mail address, password or account.
Course progressWhich sections you completed, quiz scores, time spent, and what you typed into workbook fields — so the course remembers where you are and the instructor can see where the class gets stuck.
MessagesWhat you write in the course's message panel: to the instructor, to the class, to a group, or to a classmate.
Presence marksIn courses that take attendance, the sessions you marked yourself present in.
A push-notification token (app only)If you install the Courses app and allow notifications, the app stores a device token so announcements and replies from your instructor can reach your phone. It identifies the app on your device, not you.
Page viewsWhich pages are opened, when, and a coarse country — with a random per-browser id, never your name or IP address. Used only to see which courses and pages are used.

Where it is stored

In a Google Firebase Realtime Database hosted in the European Union (Belgium, europe-west1), and in your own browser or phone. Nothing is stored on any other server.

Who can see it

  • You, on any device where you sign in with your name and code.
  • The instructor, who sees every student's name, progress, messages and presence marks for the courses he teaches.
  • Your classmates see your name in the class list of the message panel, and the messages you send to the class or to a group.

Nobody else. The data is not shared with the school, with other services, or with advertisers. Push notifications are delivered through Expo's notification service and Apple's or Google's; they carry an announcement's text or a short "your instructor replied" and nothing else.

What is not collected

No e-mail address, no password, no phone number, no precise location, no contacts, no photos, no advertising identifier. The app does not track you across other apps or websites.

Deletion

Ask the instructor — in the message panel or by e-mail — and your name, progress and messages are deleted from the database. Signing out on a page removes the saved identity from that device; uninstalling the app, or switching notifications off in its settings, removes that device's push token.
