Part C · the case · 30 marks
C1Rhône Cement: the decision memo.

Léman's client wants the industrials sleeve described as carbon neutral in next year's report. Rhône Cement — €60 m, 78 % of the sleeve's financed emissions, about 1.85 million tCO₂e a year — is the problem. Four options are on the table:

  1. Divest. Reported WACI falls 24.6 %. Real emissions: unchanged.
  2. Engage. If Rhône cuts intensity from 780 to 500 by 2030, sleeve WACI falls 9.9 % — and the tonnes are real. It takes years and may fail.
  3. Total return swap. Keep the exposure and the P&L, give away the shares, the vote and the financed emissions.
  4. Offset. Buy 1.85 Mt of credits at CHF 8; the desk judges that about 40 % of the credits are additional.

Write the memo: your recommendation, the numbers that support it, what the client may honestly claim in the report, and the one risk of your option. 300–500 words.
