The course · week by week
6–12 Jul Week 1 · Lesson

Introduction to Sustainable Investing

Take your first mandate. Define sustainable investing, place any strategy on the spectrum of capital, name the seven strategies and the PRI — and tell a credible product from greenwashing.

Start here · ~2 h 6–12 Jul Week 1 · Homework

Read the Label, Not the Cover

24 problems on the Week 1 ideas — type your answers and check them, take hints, and unlock worked solutions after an honest attempt.

~2–3 h 13–19 Jul Week 2 · Lesson

Building a Sustainable Financial System

Zoom out from the product to the machine: the market failures a system must fix, the actors and the flow of capital, and the EU-led rulebook now being built.

Open · ~2 h Forum 1 released 13–19 Jul Week 2 · Homework

Map the System

22 problems on market failures, the actors, the EU Action Plan and the building blocks — checked online, with hints and worked solutions.

~2–3 h 20–26 Jul Week 3 · Lesson

Regulatory & Policy Environment

The rules that now decide what a "sustainable" claim is allowed to mean: the EU Taxonomy, SFDR, CSRD/ESRS and the Swiss regime — and where a Geneva firm gets caught by both books.

Open · ~2 h Forum 1 open 20–26 Jul Week 3 · Homework

Read the Rulebook

22 problems on the Taxonomy, SFDR Articles 6/8/9, CSRD double materiality and the Swiss regime — checked online, with hints and worked solutions.

~2–3 h 27 Jul – 2 Aug Week 4 · Lesson

Risk Management & Measurement

Climate as a financial risk: physical, transition and liability; Scopes 1–3, financed emissions, WACI and scenario analysis; then TCFD, IFRS S2 and the nature frontier, TNFD.

Open · ~2 h Forum 1 due Sun 2 Aug 27 Jul – 2 Aug Week 4 · Homework

Put a Number on the Risk

22 problems on the risk types, the scopes, financed emissions and WACI for Léman's industrials sleeve, TCFD/ISSB and the TNFD — checked online, with hints and worked solutions.

~2–3 h 3–9 Aug Week 5 · Lesson

The Sustainable Debt Market

Green, social, sustainability and sustainability-linked bonds; the ICMA Principles; the greenium and its break-even; the EU Green Bond Standard — and the mechanics behind the Apple case.

Open · ~2 h Online Test 1 this week 3–9 Aug Week 5 · Homework

Read the Term Sheet

22 problems on the four structures, the ICMA components, SLB step-up arithmetic, the greenium break-even and the 85% EuGB test — checked online, with hints and worked solutions.

~2–3 h 10–16 Aug Week 6 · Lesson

The Sustainable Equity Market

Equity gives you no contract — only a screen and a vote. The five strategies from exclusion to impact, SFDR Article 8 vs 9, carbon intensity and what a screen costs in tracking error, stewardship and the escalation ladder — and the divest-or-engage decision on Rhône Cement, priced both ways.

Open · ~2 h Forum 2 released 10–16 Aug Week 6 · Checkpoint

Timed Quiz · Sustainable Equity

18 questions, 30 minutes, two attempts and your better score counts. Open book, password required — it is in the Week 6 announcement. Set on a different fund from the lesson, so it tests the method rather than recall. Worked solutions unlock when it closes on 16 August.

⏱ 30 min 10–16 Aug Week 6 · Homework

Own It, or Sell It

21 problems on reading a factsheet for what it really promises, SFDR classification, weighted-average carbon intensity arithmetic, the exclusion trade-off, stewardship leverage and the divest-or-engage decision — checked online, with hints and worked solutions.
