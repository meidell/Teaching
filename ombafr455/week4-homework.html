Financed emissions = (position ÷ enterprise value) × company emissions, summed.
Carbon intensity = company emissions ÷ company revenue, in tCO₂e per €m.
WACI = Σ (position weight × company carbon intensity).

Careful with units: emissions are given in kilotonnes (kt) and 1 kt = 1,000 tCO₂e. All figures are rounded, illustrative numbers for the Week 4 exercises.
