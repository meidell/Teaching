FALL WEEK 1 · HOMEWORK · ~2 HOURS · CALCULATOR ALLOWED · NOT GRADED

The ten problems from the printed sheet

These are Homework Week 1 exactly as handed out — same problems, same numbers, same order. A few carry more than one field because the printed version asks for more than one thing. Work each one by hand first, type your answer, and check it. Stuck? Take a hint. Then read the full solution — that is where the learning is.

Round to two decimals unless told otherwise. Use the calculator you will sit the exam with: a Casio FX-82 Solar or a TI-30 eco RS. Getting fluent with its keys now is worth marks in January.

📌 How this homework is marked — it isn't

Homework in this course does not count toward your grade. Neither does the weekly review quiz. That is deliberate: they exist so that you, and your instructor, find out what has not landed before the midterm in Week 9, at a moment when it is free to fix.

What does count: 20% fall midterm (MCQ, 60 min) · 40% fall exam · 40% spring exam. Both finals are 120 minutes, closed book, open questions — one calculator and two hand-written sheets recto/verso. Everything you do here is rehearsal for that room.
