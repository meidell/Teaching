Fall Week 9 · Quantitative Methods I · HEG Genève

The midterm — and then, differentiation in reverse

Week 9 is two halves. The first is the midterm exam: 60 minutes, multiple choice, 20% of the module grade. The second is a short course opening the last block of the fall semester — integration, which is the derivative run backwards, and the tool that recovers a total from a rate.

🏫 In class: 3 h · lesson, then exercises 📖 Midterm (MCQ, 60 min) · antiderivatives ▶ The Lecture button shows the slide outline

By the end of Fall Week 9 you will be able to

  • Sit the midterm: 60 minutes, multiple choice, covering Weeks 1 to 8 including the self-study week.
  • Understand integration as the reverse of differentiation.
  • Compute simple antiderivatives.
  • Recover total cost and revenue from marginal functions.
