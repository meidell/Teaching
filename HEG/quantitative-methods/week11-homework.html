FALL WEEK 11 · HOMEWORK · ~1 HOUR · NO CALCULATOR NEEDED · NOT GRADED

The seven problems from the printed sheet

These are Homework exercises Week 11 exactly as handed out: the same problems, the same order. The sheet asks you to name things, a sampling method, a data timing, a level of measurement, and to explain. A form cannot mark an explanation, so each part is a choice: pick the name from the list, and write your reason on paper before you check. The worked solution carries the full explanation, which is what the exam will ask you to write. Work each problem by hand first, choose your answers and press Check. If you are stuck, take a hint. Then read the worked solution, also when your answer was right.

📌 How this homework is marked — it isn't

Homework in this course does not count toward your grade. Neither does the weekly review quiz. They exist so that you, and your instructor, find out what you cannot do yet before the fall exam, while there is still time to fix it.

What does count: 20% fall midterm (MCQ, 60 min) · 40% fall exam · 40% spring exam. Both finals are 120 minutes, closed book, open questions — one calculator and two hand-written sheets recto/verso. Everything you do here is rehearsal for that room.
