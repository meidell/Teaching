Fall Week 5 · Quantitative Methods I · HEG Genève

The toolkit, drilled

A short, honest week: the rules that let you differentiate anything this course will hand you. Product, quotient and chain — the chain rule most of all, because in business almost every function is a function of a function.

🏫 In class: 3 h · lesson, then exercises 📖 Product · quotient · chain rules · higher-order derivatives ▶ The Lecture button shows the slide outline

By the end of Fall Week 5 you will be able to

  • Differentiate the standard functions quickly and correctly.
  • Apply the rules of differentiation to composite expressions.

Section 5.3 · The product rule

When two things vary at once

Planned · Product

(fg)′ = f′g + fg′

  • Revenue = price × quantity, with both varying: the product rule IS the economics of a price change.
  • Worked: R(p) = p·q(p), and the derivative read as "the extra francs per unit minus the units lost".

Section 5.4 · The quotient rule

Rates of rates

Planned · Quotient

(f/g)′, and average cost

  • Average cost A(q) = C(q)/q differentiated, and the classic result: average cost is flat exactly where marginal cost crosses it.
  • That is the Week 1 roastery table, proved rather than observed.
