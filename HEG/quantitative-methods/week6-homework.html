Business Case Week 6 · Luxury Coffee Co: a quantitative study

The review case for the self-study week, question for question — the same eighteen questions as the case on Cyberlearn, in the same order, under the case's own headings. Where a question asks you to express a function, the fields ask you to evaluate it at a stated point: if your function is right, the numbers will be. Where it asks you to explain, or to write the memo, write your answer first and then choose the one the numbers support. Work each question by hand first, type your answer, and check it. Stuck? Take a hint. Then read the solution: it starts from the printed answer and adds the working the answer sheet leaves out.

Type numbers without spaces (150000, not 150 000). Round to two decimals unless told otherwise. Use the calculator you will sit the exam with: a Casio FX-82 Solar or a TI-30 eco RS.

📌 How this homework is marked — it isn't

The case does not count toward your grade. It exists so that you find out what has not landed before the midterm in Week 9, at a moment when it is free to fix. If a question stops you, section 6.4 of the Week 6 page names the week and section that taught it.

What does count: 20% fall midterm (MCQ, 60 min) · 40% fall exam · 40% spring exam. Both finals are 120 minutes, closed book, open questions — one calculator and two hand-written sheets recto/verso. Everything you do here is rehearsal for that room.
