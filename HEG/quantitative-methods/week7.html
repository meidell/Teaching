Fall Week 7 · Quantitative Methods I · HEG Genève

Elasticity, growth, and how much a price cut really costs

The derivative earns its keep. Marginal analysis on all three functions, price elasticity of demand — the number that decides whether a discount makes or loses money — growth rates, linear approximation, and Newton's method for the equations that will not solve.

🏫 In class: 3 h · lesson, then exercises 📖 Marginal analysis · elasticity · linear approximation · Newton ▶ The Lecture button shows the slide outline

By the end of Fall Week 7 you will be able to

  • Measure change, margins and growth quantitatively.
  • Compute and interpret marginal cost, marginal revenue and marginal profit.
  • Compute and interpret elasticity and growth rates.
  • Solve more complicated equations with an iterative method.
  • Approximate functions with linear approximations.
