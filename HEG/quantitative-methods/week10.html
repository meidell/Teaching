Fall Week 10 · Quantitative Methods I · HEG Genève

Area, accumulation, and what the customer got for free

The definite integral as area and as accumulated change — and then the idea it was invented for in economics: consumer and producer surplus, the value a market creates beyond the price that changes hands.

🏫 In class: 3 h · lesson, then exercises 📖 The definite integral · area · consumer and producer surplus ▶ The Lecture button shows the slide outline

By the end of Fall Week 10 you will be able to

  • Understand integration as the reverse of differentiation and as accumulation.
  • Compute definite integrals and interpret the area under a curve.
  • Compute and interpret surpluses.
