The fifteen problems from the printed sheet

These are Homework exercises Week 10 exactly as handed out: the same problems, the same numbers, the same order, including the two review problems at the end. Where the sheet asks for an indefinite integral, a form cannot mark the function you find, so the fields ask either for the number in front of your answer, or for F(1) − F(0): put x = 1 and x = 0 into your antiderivative and subtract. That number is the same for every correct antiderivative, because the + C cancels. Work each problem by hand first, type your answer and press Check. If you are stuck, take a hint. Then read the worked solution, also when your answer was right.

Round to three decimals unless told otherwise. Use the calculator you will sit the exam with: a Casio FX-82 Solar or a TI-30 eco RS. Getting fluent with its keys now is worth marks in January.

📌 How this homework is marked — it isn't

Homework in this course does not count toward your grade. Neither does the weekly review quiz. They exist so that you, and your instructor, find out what you cannot do yet before the fall exam, while there is still time to fix it.

What does count: 20% fall midterm (MCQ, 60 min) · 40% fall exam · 40% spring exam. Both finals are 120 minutes, closed book, open questions — one calculator and two hand-written sheets recto/verso. Everything you do here is rehearsal for that room.
