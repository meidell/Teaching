Week 12 · Applied Statistics · HEG Genève

Everything from Weeks 1–10, in twelve lines

The exam is by hand; the job is not. This week installs R and RStudio and redoes the semester in code: describe the thirty prices, test the CHF 20 claim, compare the lake and the station, fit the price–rating line, run the chi-square — and get the same numbers you got with a calculator. Computer session; bring a laptop.

🏫 In class: Week 12 📖 Install R ▶ The Lecture button shows the slide outline

By the end of Week 12 you will be able to

  • Install R and RStudio and run a script.
  • Load a small dataset and compute mean, median, sd, quantiles; draw a histogram and a box plot.
  • Run t.test, prop.test, chisq.test and lm — and match each output to the hand calculation from earlier weeks.
  • Read an R output without panic: statistic, df, p-value, confidence interval.
  • Keep a script that reproduces your project's analysis.
