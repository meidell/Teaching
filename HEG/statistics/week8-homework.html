Thirteen exercises from the book

Every problem here is an end-of-section exercise from Saylor's Introductory Statistics, chapter 9, reproduced word for word and labelled with its section and number — the ones we did not do in class. Type each answer, check it, take a hint if stuck, then read the full solution. Take zα/2 and tα/2 to three decimals from the book's t table (Figure 12.3) ↗ — its last row, ∞ [z], gives every z you need — take t from the row df = n₁ + n₂ − 2 (pooled) or n − 1 (paired), keep every standard error unrounded, and round the margin the way the book's answer does. Decide the design first: independent or paired. Where the book's key slips on a sign, the solution says so. Parts that ask you to verify, comment or interpret are answered in the solution.
