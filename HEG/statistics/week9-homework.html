Thirteen exercises from the book

Every problem here is an end-of-section exercise from Saylor's Introductory Statistics, chapter 10, reproduced word for word and labelled with its section and number — the ones we did not do in class. Type each answer, check it, take a hint if stuck, then read the full solution. Take tα/2 to three decimals from the book's t table (Figure 12.3) ↗ on the row df = n − 2. Keep SSxx, SSxy, SSyy and β̂1 unrounded until the end: several problems reuse the numbers of an earlier one, as the book's exercises do. Parts that ask you to verify, comment or interpret are answered in the solution.
