Week 6 · Applied Statistics · HEG Genève

The Geneva price — with a margin

Week 5 told you how far a sample mean wanders. Now turn that around: from one sample, build an interval that captures the true Geneva price with a stated confidence. Large samples use z; small samples use t; proportions have their own formula; and the last section asks the question a budget asks — how many pizzerias must we visit to be within one franc?

🏫 In class: 3 h · lesson ~1 h, then exercises ~2 h 📖 Saylor ch. 7 ▶ The Lecture button shows the slide outline

By the end of Week 6 you will be able to

  • Build and interpret a confidence interval for a mean with a large sample: x̄ ± z·σ/√n.
  • Build one for a small sample with t and df = n − 1 — and say why t is wider.
  • Build a confidence interval for a proportion: p̂ ± z·√(p̂q̂/n).
  • Compute the sample size needed for a target margin of error.
  • Say what "95% confident" means — and what it does not.
