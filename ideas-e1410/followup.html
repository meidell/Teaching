After the course

Six weeks on

The course is over and this is when most of it evaporates. Six prompts, one a week, each one a situation your own project would actually hit — using what you wrote in your workbooks. Ten minutes each. They are not assessed, nobody is marking them, and they are the difference between having attended a course and having changed how you work.

Why this exists. Everything we know about executive education says the same thing: people retain what they retrieve, not what they hear. Four weeks after a course, most of it is gone unless something pulled it back out. This is that something — and it uses your project rather than a case study, because the retrieval only works if the thing you are recalling is real.
