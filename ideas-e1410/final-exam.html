How this works

  • You are signed in as —. Your answers are recorded under that name and this is your one attempt.
  • 45 seconds per question. When the timer runs out the question locks and scores zero.
  • One attempt per question, and no going back. Once a question has been shown it is finished with.
  • No answers are revealed during the exam. Your result appears the moment you finish; the full question-by-question review unlocks once the exam has closed for everyone.
  • You may stop and resume. The exam resumes at the next unseen question, on this device or any other you sign in from — but the clock on a question you abandon does not stop.
The one thing to know before you start: a question counts the moment it appears. If you close the tab, reload, or leave in the middle of one, that question is spent and scores zero. Finish the question you are on before you go anywhere.
