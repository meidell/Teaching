Is this a WBS you can trust?

A work breakdown structure is trustworthy when nothing is missing, every leaf can be estimated and owned, and a stranger reads each line the same way you do. Two of those three are checkable by machine. Type your breakdown in; this numbers it, rolls up the effort, and tells you where it does not hold — including the things AI projects leave out every single time.

What the structure tells me

A machine cannot judge whether your scope is complete — that needs someone who knows the project. It can judge shape, sizing and the omissions that repeat across every AI project ever planned.

    The things AI projects forget

    Not a rule, a pattern. These are the work packages that are missing from most first-draft breakdowns — and they are missing from the budget too, because the WBS is what the budget is built from. Green means I found something in your WBS that looks like it; red means I found nothing.

    A red chip is not automatically wrong. Some projects genuinely have no migration, no decommissioning, no regulatory exposure. But you should be able to say why — “not applicable because…” is a finished thought; silence is not.
