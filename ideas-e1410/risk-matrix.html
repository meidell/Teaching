The risk register that argues back

Rating a risk 3×3 and moving on is the most common thing a project manager does and the least useful. Put your risks in here and the page plots them, ranks them, and then asks the questions a good steering committee asks: who owns this, what would you see first, and why is every risk you've listed a technical one?
