How this works

  • You are signed in as —. Your answers are recorded under that name.
  • 30 seconds per question. When the timer runs out the question locks and counts as unanswered.
  • One attempt per question, and no going back. Once a question has been shown, it is finished with — right, wrong or out of time.
  • You may stop and come back. The quiz resumes at the next unseen question, on this device or any other you sign in from.
  • Every answer is explained immediately, and the full review waits for you at the end.
The one thing to know before you start: a question counts the moment it appears. If you close the tab, reload, or leave in the middle of one, that question is spent and scores zero. Finish the question you are on before you go anywhere.
