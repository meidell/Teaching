All four, on the same numbers

Every measure below is computed from the same one hundred thousand MekongPay transactions: 200 of them fraud, the model catching 180, and raising 2,176 alerts in total.

MekongPay, one day of traffic scaled to 100,000 transactions of which 200 are fraud, at the targets you have been writing since Session 1: catch 90% of fraud, keep false alarms under 2%.

  • Accuracy — everything it got right, over everything. (180 + 97,804) / 100,000 = 98.0%. Looks like a success.
  • Sensitivity / recall — of the real fraud, what we caught. 180 / 200 = 90.0%. Target met.
  • Specificity — of the genuine transactions, what we left alone. 97,804 / 99,800 = 98.0%. Target met.
  • Precision — of the alerts we raised, what was real. 180 / 2,176 = 8.3%. Nobody set a target, and this is the one that decides whether the fraud team can function.

Which one do you quote, and to whom

To the COO: sensitivity, because it maps to money not lost. To Customer Support: specificity and the absolute number of blocked customers. To the fraud team who has to work the queue: precision, always — it is their whole day.

Quoting accuracy to any of them tells them nothing, and quoting a measure that flatters you to an audience that will later discover the others is how a project loses its sponsor.

What you should be able to say afterwards
Takeaways
  • Sensitivity and recall are the same measure. Two names, one calculation — do not let the vocabulary convince you there is a third concept.
  • Precision and recall share a numerator and differ in denominator. Recall divides by everything that was truly positive; precision divides by everything you flagged.
  • When positives are rare, precision is the one that hurts. That is the whole of the Session 5 trap in a sentence.
On MekongPay → Take your own Session 1 workbook to the compile page and read your technical success metric again. If it names only how much of the target class you catch, you have specified sensitivity and left precision unspecified — which is exactly how MekongPay reached a stage gate with 92% of its alerts wrong and every stated target met.
