    Which "chapter" is which — the one table that reconciles them

    Two numbering systems run side by side, and they do not line up. The syllabus numbers the course's eleven subjects 1 to 11 — and confusingly calls them "chapters" when it states what a test covers. Doane & Seward, the prescribed textbook, has its own chapter numbers, and those are what the Required Reading column of the syllabus gives you. Week 4 is syllabus topic 6 and Doane chapter 8; both are correct, they are just counting different things. (The free OpenStax text these lessons also quote adds a third numbering — it is always labelled "OpenStax" wherever it appears.)

    Every week page carries both numbers in its header, so you can always tell which system a page is speaking. Source: Syllabus Part 2, §4 Learning schedule. If Moodle and this site ever disagree, message me — Moodle's assessment dates and scope are the ones that count.
