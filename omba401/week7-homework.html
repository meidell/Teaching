The line, and how much to believe it

Twenty-four problems on everything Week 7 taught: building a least-squares line from five raw sums, saying what the slope and intercept mean in francs, telling r from R² and both from the standard error, reading residuals, testing whether a slope is real, choosing the right interval for a prediction, and fitting a multiple regression with a dummy variable. All inside Café Léman and its twelve cafés.

How this works: work each problem by hand or in Excel, type your answer, then Check. Stuck? Take a hint — no penalty. The full worked solution unlocks only after your first check. Numeric answers: round as instructed; comma or dot decimals both work. Give money in CHF without the currency symbol, and give r, R² and adjusted R² as decimals (e.g. 0.9503), not percentages.
