Trend, season and how wrong you are

Twenty-three problems on everything Week 8 taught: reading a seasonal series without being fooled by it, building a centred moving average, turning ratios into normalised seasonal indices, deseasonalising and fitting a trend that means something, reseasonalising a forecast, and scoring it against a benchmark. All on the Nyon café's three years of monthly revenue.

How this works: work each problem by hand or in Excel, type your answer, then Check. Stuck? Take a hint — no penalty. The full worked solution unlocks only after your first check. Numeric answers: round as instructed; comma or dot decimals both work. Give money in CHF without the symbol or thousands separators, percentages as numbers (e.g. −20.1 for a 20.1% fall, keeping the sign), and seasonal indices to 4 decimals. Your progress, score and time sync to your professor's dashboard once you've signed in.
